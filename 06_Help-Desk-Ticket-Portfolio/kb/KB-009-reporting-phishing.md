# KB-009: Suspicious email (phishing)

**Audience:** all staff and help desk · **Tickets:** HD-1014

## Staff: what to do

1. **Don't click links, open attachments, or reply.**
2. Click **Report > Report phishing** in Outlook (or forward it as an attachment to the help desk).
3. Delete it.
4. **If you already clicked or typed your password:** call the help desk right away. Speed matters more than embarrassment; it happens to everyone.

Warning signs: urgent requests about pay, passwords or gift cards; a sender address that doesn't match the name; links whose address doesn't match the text when you hover.

## Help desk: response steps (Priority 1)

1. Thank the reporter. Get the email as an attachment, with its headers.
2. **Find who else got it** (message trace) and whether anyone clicked or replied.
3. **Anyone who entered a password:** reset it now (KB-001), sign them out of all sessions, and check that their MFA methods haven't been changed.
4. **Block** the sender and the link domain. **Remove** the email from all mailboxes.
5. Send a short all-staff warning with a screenshot.
6. Escalate to the network administrator. Record the timeline in the ticket: received, reported, contained.
