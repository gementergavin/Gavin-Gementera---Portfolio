# KB-004: Classroom projector or TV shows "No signal"

**Audience:** teachers and help desk · **Tickets:** HD-1003

Most "no signal" problems are fixed in under 2 minutes with these steps. Try them in order.

1. **Is the display on the right input?** Press the **Input/Source** button on the remote or panel and choose the HDMI port the cable is plugged into (HDMI 1, HDMI 2...).
2. **Is the laptop sending a picture?** On Windows press **Win + P** and choose **Duplicate**. On a Chromebook press **Ctrl + Full-screen** (the rectangle with two lines) to mirror.
3. **Reseat the cable** at both ends: laptop and wall plate. If there's an adapter (USB-C to HDMI), unplug and replug it.
4. **Try another device** (a phone with an adapter, or a colleague's laptop). If that works, the problem is the laptop. If not, it's the cable, wall plate or display.
5. **Restart the display:** power it off at the button, wait 10 seconds, power it on.

## When to submit a ticket

If none of these work, submit a ticket with the room number, the display brand, and what you tried. While you wait, the help desk can bring a loaner projector cart.

## Help desk notes

Bring a known-good HDMI cable and a USB-C adapter. If a different device works on the same cable, check the laptop's display driver and Windows display settings. Log any broken wall plate for facilities.
