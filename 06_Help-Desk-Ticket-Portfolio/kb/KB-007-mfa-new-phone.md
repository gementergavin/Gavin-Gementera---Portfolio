# KB-007: Setting up sign-in verification (MFA) on a new phone

**Audience:** staff · **Tickets:** HD-1013

District staff accounts use multi-factor authentication (MFA): your password plus a code or approval from the Authenticator app on your phone.

## Moving to a new phone

**If you still have the old phone:** sign in to your account's security info page on a computer, choose **Add sign-in method** > Authenticator app, and follow the steps to scan the QR code with the new phone. Then remove the old phone from the list.

**If you don't have the old phone:** contact the help desk. We'll verify your identity and clear your old method so you can set up the new one.

## Help desk

1. **Verify identity first** (see KB-001). Clearing MFA for the wrong person hands them the account.
2. In the identity admin portal, find the user > Authentication methods > **Require re-register MFA** (or delete the old method).
3. Stay on the line while they sign in and register the new phone.
4. Note in the ticket how you verified them.
