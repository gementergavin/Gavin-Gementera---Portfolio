# KB-006: Chromebook won't turn on or charge

**Audience:** students, teachers, help desk · **Tickets:** HD-1006, HD-1018

## Students and teachers: try these first

1. **Charge it for 30 minutes** with a charger you know works. Check the light next to the charging port: amber is charging, white or green is full, no light means no power is getting in.
2. **Hard reset:** hold **Refresh** (the circular arrow key) and press **Power**. Release Refresh when it starts. This doesn't erase anything.
3. If the screen stays black but you hear sounds or see lights, the screen may be broken; bring it to the library or tech help desk.

## Help desk

1. Swap the charger. A bad charger is the most common cause.
2. Check for physical damage: cracked screen, bent port, liquid.
3. Still dead: **log a repair in the [device tracker](../../08_Chromebook-Device-Tracker)**, give the student a loaner, and note the loaner's asset tag in this ticket.

## Updates before testing days (HD-1018)

Chromebooks update themselves when charged and online. Before state testing:

1. Charge the whole cart overnight.
2. Power each one on and sign in, or leave on the sign-in screen on Wi-Fi for 30 minutes.
3. Settings > About ChromeOS > **Check for updates** > Restart.
4. A device that won't update or sign in may need a **powerwash** (factory reset): it re-enrolls automatically if the district manages it.
